Stockholm is easy to access by public transport, and on bicycle during the warm season. Travelling by car is suitable outside built-up areas.
- By public transport

[SL](https://sl.se/en/in-english) (Storstockholms Lokaltrafik) runs an extensive metro, commuter train and bus system, as well as some light rail and ferry services. They serve Stockholm County, with Sigtuna and Norrtälje in the north, and Södertälje and Nynäshamn in the south, with extensions to Uppsala, Gnesta and Bålsta for an extra fee (see below).

The SL website has up-to-date detailed ticket and price information in English, and a journey planner.
- Tickets

The simplest way to pay for tickets is to simply use your credit card to pay for tickets, by tapping at entry gates and electronic readers. Tickets cannot be bought on buses by cash. As you board a public transport vehicle (most prominently this applies to buses), you will be required to validate your trip every time by swiping the payment card at the rectangular validators in the vehicle (you should hear a beep and see a green light). The same principle goes for the underground and commuter rail, with the main difference being that this procedure is done as you're entering the platform part of the station. For light rail, there are validators on the platforms.

All SL services use an integrated ticket system with an RFID card (SL-kortet), which triggers entry gates and other electronic readers. As of July 2026 the card costs 50 kr to purchase and can hold all types of SL tickets.

There are two forms of ticketing, passes and coupons. Passes and coupons can be bought at any metro or commuter rail station, at SL Center (located at Sergels torg, close to the central station/T-centralen), at all convenience stores (such as Pressbyrån or 7-11), most supermarkets and some smaller kiosks. Passes are available for 24 hours, 72 hours, 7 days, and 30 days, and allow unrestricted rides on all buses, trams, metro, and commuter trains (outside of Arlanda Central and stations north of it), as well as the Djurgården ferry. A coupon ticket allows unlimited travel for 75 minutes including changes.
