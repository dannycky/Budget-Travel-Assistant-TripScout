# Saipan — Budget Travel Guide

## Commute Tips
There is no public transport as such, excluding shuttle buses running between hotels and the Duty Free shopping center in Garapan.
- By bus

There is no public bus service on Saipan, but on Saipan PDI and other companies run a shopping shuttle bus to and from the Duty Free mall (DFS Galleria) in Garapan. DFS also has their own free shuttle from most of the major hotels.
- By taxi

Taxis on Saipan are expensive but plentiful, however the only place they are allowed to pick up or drop off passengers is at the airport or a hotel, and sometimes at DFS. There is also a large network of illegal taxis run by Chinese immigrants, that will take you anywhere for $15. For some Saipan residents this is their only means of transportation.

Because Saipan is so small, taxis from the airport to the main hotel area of Garapan can run upwards of $25-30, so plan ahead by arranging transportation through your hotel, which often charge $10 per person flat rate.
- By car

Car rental is a good option for getting off the beaten track, as roads are generally quite good.   Most car rental offices are either at the airport or at major tourist hotels; some offer a free pick up and drop off service.
- By moped

Mopeds can be rented for about $20, though roads are very dark at night and bugs predominate after dusk.  Therefore, consider mopeds for day-tripping only, as drivers tend not to give heed to mopeds or bicyclists throughout the Northern Mariana Islands or Guam.

## Sightseeing

### History
- **American Memorial Park** (Free) — The only NPS park in the Northern Mariana Islands, honoring those who sacrificed their lives during the Pacific War against Japan in the battles that took place in Saipan, Tinian, and the Philippine Sea in 1944. [Website](http://www.nps.gov/amme/)

### Sports
- **Aquasmith** — American, Chamorro Japanese instructors. Dive shop (scuba diving, skin diving, spearfishing). [Website](http://www.aquasmith.com/)

### Nature
- **Marianas Trekking** — Mountain bike tours, ATV tours, and kayak tours and all of it is done completely first rate. The guides are super friendly, the equipment is good, and the fun factor and local knowledge is definitely better than anything you could do on your own.

### Other
- **Tripper's Tours** — Canadian dive guide, instructor, and underwater photographer & videographer with 15 years' experience, including 7 on Saipan. [Website](http://saipanscuba.blogspot.com)
- **Dive Saipan** — Korean dive shop [Website](https://divesaipan3076.com/)
- **Speedy Tertle** — Russian dive shop
- **G.O.D.I.** — Dive shop. Russian-, Japanese- and English-speaking.

## Budget Eats
- **Canton Restaurant** — Fantastic Chinese food and excellent service. Delicious roast duck and excellent steamed parrotfish. Oldest Chinese restaurant on the island.
- **Coffee Care** — Has excellent coffee and some very good homemade pasta dishes, sandwiches, soup, and desserts.
- **Ebisuya** — Fresh bread, sandwiches, sushi, and homemade soup.
- **Esko's** — One of the few local places catering locally made treats dating back to the Spanish era. Excellent place to experience the real taste of the Marianas.
- **Himawari** — Sushi, bread, pastries, and handmade fresh noodle.
- **Wild Bill's Bar & Grill** — Great omelets, hash browns, and pancakes. [Website](https://www.facebook.com/wildbillsaipan)
- **Winchell's** — For donuts and coffee.
- **Bobby Cadillac's** — A casual deli and pizza spot with a bar. Hot pastrami on rye with melted Swiss cheese!

## Budget Stays
- **Gold Beach Hotel**
- **Saipan Skyline Designers Hotel** (From $50) — A small hotel in the mountains of the northern part of the island. Reportedly has a shuttle bus, but a car is essential if staying here. The rooms are nothing special, but the views are unbeatable. Prices are reasonable. Only bookable through online agencies, like Hotels.com.
- **Saipan World Resort** — Susupe. Massive hotel with all the facilities you'd expect set on a beautiful beach. The best thing to do here is to borrow a kayak (free), paddle out to the rusting tank hunks a few hundred meters offshore, tie up your boat and dive in to see the corals and fish lurking nearby. World resort stay in [Website](https://www.saipanworldresort.com/en/index.do)
- **Fiesta Resort and Spa Saipan** — Coral Tree Avenue, Garapan. Located in the heart of Saipan's Tourism district, on the white sands of Micro Beach, near shopping centers and nightlife, the Fiesta Resort and Spa, Saipan is a leading resort in the Northern Mariana Islands. [Website](http://www.fiestasaipan.com)

## Budget Drinks
- **Oleai Beach Club** — A great 'sunset' bar and lunch spot on the beach in San Jose Village. It's popular with the locals because of their lunchtime $1 Taco Special.
- **Hash House Harriers** — The Saipan Hash House Harriers meet every Saturday at the Bank of Guam building in Garapan at 3:30PM in Winter, and 4PM in Summer. Join the Hash if you'd like to meet people from Saipan, see some of the lesser known areas of the island, and enjoy a bit of outrageous partying. The cost is US$10 per p [Website](http://www.huffypuffy.com)
- **Karaoke/Hostess Bars** — abound in Garapan all of which allow ample drinking opportunities if one is so inclined.
