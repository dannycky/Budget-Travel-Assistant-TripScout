Ningbo is so much bigger than the small downtown area, staying downtown can fill a few days but it's just like any major city. To really see some jaw dropping parts of Ningbo, you have to adventure into the outer districts. There are breathtaking mountain ranges steeped in a deep and meaningful history that has been relatively undisturbed.
- Metro: Like many Chinese cities, Ningbo is rapidly expanding its metro system. Line  with 29 stations opened in early 2014. Line  with 27 stations opened in 2015. Line  with 15 stations opened in mid 2019. Lines  with 25 stations opened in 2020, and  with 22 stations opened in 2021.
- Taxis are ¥10 for the first 3&nbsp;km plus ¥2 fuel tax.
- Buses are ¥1 (¥2 with air conditioning).
- Rental cars: Are available at Ningbo Lishe International Airport.
- Motor bikes: Although Downtown Ningbo does not permit motorbikes a bike can be rented outside of downtown and can make an enjoyable way of reaching the outer districts.

## Budget Eats
- **Mezzos on 2** — Swanky Italian restaurant with 5-star interior decoration.
- **Picanha Brazilian BBQ** (¥228 for meat/buffet/unlimited beer or ¥128 for meat/buffet) — Pretty good Brazilian BBQ, buffet style. - **Banana Leaf Curry House** — Opposite the Shamrock Irish Pub. The English-speaking staff gladly advises on their offerings and are more than happy tosurprise you with a selection of their meals.
- **Pizza Pino** — The only place in Ningbo with a wood-fired oven but ruins the pizza by not using buffalo mozzarella.
- **Da Bossi Ristorante** — A very fine and fancy Italian. Get a group together and enjoy an open fire in a private dining rooms on the 3rd floor. Book ahead as it gets busy.

- **Spices** — Indian food, a huge menu. The decoration is appealing and the service is a bit slow but still they have a very good menu with nice pictures to select from. You can order a delivery as well with them.
