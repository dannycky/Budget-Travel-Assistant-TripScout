## Commute Tips
Niigata has a bus system that is very good, but also its very easy to walk everywhere, since most of the main attractions are within walking distance of each other.

Most major sights in Niigata can be easily accessed by bicycle. Rentals are provided by the city from several locations scattered throughout and rental fees are ¥100 for the first 3 hours and ¥100 for each additional hour. If you plan on keeping the bicycle longer than 4 hours, the trick is to return the bicycle at a nearby rental outlet and check it out again before your first 3 hours are up. In this way, your rental is charged the ¥100 rate each 3 hours.  The tourist information office in the Niigata train station can supply you with the necessary maps and show you the closest rental office &mdash; about 5 minutes walk. The rental location can also supply you with maps that show you all the rental outlets. ID is needed to rent a bike.  Be sure to check the brakes, tire pressure, and how to use the bike lock before you leave the rental outlet.

## Sightseeing

### Art
- **Minatopia - Niigata City History Museum** (¥300 adults, ¥200 college or vocational school students, ¥100 elementary, junior) — A museum where you can learn about the history and culture of Niigata. Audio guides available in English, Korean, and Russian. [Website](https://www.nchm.jp/)
- **Northern Culture Museum** (¥800 adult, ¥400 children) — Built as a wealthy family's home in 1885-1887. The museum comprises several buildings, and a few restaurants/cafes. Documents and art collections including paintings and sculptures from former households are on display. It is worth at least a half day visit. [Website](http://hoppou-bunka.com/)
- **The Nakano Residence - Museum of Art Foundation** (¥700 adults, ¥500 college or vocational school students, ¥100 elementary, junior) [Website](http://www.nakanotei-muse.com/)

### Sports
- **Denka Big Swan Stadium** — Home of the Albirex Niigata football team. [Website](https://www.denka-bigswan.com)
- **Shinji Mizushima Manga Street** — A street with bronze artwork statues of Mizushima Baseball manga players. Back in 2015, "Ketsu Bat Girl!” photo trend originated here.

### Nature
- **Toki Messe Observation Deck** (Free) — Toki Messe is a modern multi-use convention center. The observation deck on the top floor is a tourist spot where you can observe a panoramic view of Niigata City. [Website](http://www.nikkoniigata.com/temboushitsu/)
- **Bandai Bridge** — Built in 1929 at the site of the first bridge to cross the Shinano River, this bridge is a nationally Important Cultural Property. [Website](https://www.hrr.mlit.go.jp/niikoku/bandaibridge/)
- **Shinano River Cruise** — You can see the scenery of Niigata City from the waterbus that runs along the Shinano River. There is also an open-air deck where passengers can relax and enjoy the view from the river outside. There are three cruises a day.

### Other
- **Round 1 - Niigata** — Indoor amusement center with arcade batting cages, bowling, karaoke, squash, mini golf, etc. [Website](http://www.round1.co.jp/shop/tenpo/niigata-niigata.html)
