## Commute Tips
There is a Visit Hiroshima Tourist Pass that covers either 1-3 days of unlimited journeys inside the city or the prefecture. It can be obtained from the Information Center on the first floor of the airport, the JR station, the Hiroshima Bus Center, and other tourist locations.  Digital passes are also available [online](https://www.mobirytravel.jp/) and are suitable for travelers seeking to use public transportation frequently during their trip in the city.
- By tram

Hiroshima has an extensive tram (streetcar) network, which is operated by [Hiroden](http://www.hiroden.co.jp/en/) (広電). It's a slow but reliable way of getting around. The trams themselves are a mix of old rattle-traps and sleek, new "Green Movers" &mdash; although they all run on the same lines for the same fares. There's no difference other than the smoothness of the ride. Because the trams were bought from other cities, you're getting a tour of Japanese transit history &mdash; some have been in service for more than fifty years, and that might be an old Kyoto tram taking you through Hiroshima. Trams 651 and 652, both of which survived the atomic bomb blast, are still sometimes used in regular service.

Most lines originate from JR Hiroshima Station, and run frequently during daytime and evening hours, approximately one tram every 10 minutes per line. Boarding and payment procedures vary by tram; however, the entrance and exit are clearly marked in English. (If in doubt, just follow the locals.) Pay as you exit. Change machines are usually available on board if you don't have exact change &mdash; check near the front or back of the car. A of Nov 2025, trips are a flat ¥240 (¥120 for children ages 6-11; up to 3 children ages 1-5 may ride with a paying adult/older child). One-day passes are available from the tourist office for ¥700 (¥350 children). A day pass which also includes travel on the Matsudai Kisen ferry to Miyajima costs ¥1000 (¥550 children). Digital passes are also available [online](https://www.mobirytravel.jp/) and include 8/24 hour passes for ¥600/700 (¥300/350 children) and weekday (10:00-16:00) and weekend (8 hours) passes for ¥440 (¥220 children).
